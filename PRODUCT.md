# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite (user choice).

## Users

Mixed, general audience: anyone who lands on the site, including recruiters, potential collaborators or clients, and academic contacts. Most visitors skim quickly and need to understand who the owner is, what they build, and where the proof is.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for an Industrial Engineering student who also builds full-stack web apps and finance/data projects. It exists to show breadth backed by real, working projects. Success means a visitor leaves knowing the owner's mix of skills and has opened at least one project.

## Positioning

A blend of three identities, none subordinate: **Industrial Engineering student**, **full-stack web developer**, and **finance/data builder**. The distinctive angle is the combination: engineering and operations thinking (supply chain, demand forecasting) applied through software the owner builds end to end.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Shows only the three projects the owner chose: Demand Forecaster, SEC Filing Analyzer, Supply Chain Analytics. Other workspace projects stay off the site unless the owner adds them.
- Each project links to its GitHub repo. CV (including phone number, approved by the owner) is downloadable at `public/Ates-Parilti-CV.pdf`. No live demos yet; Dutch version undecided.

## Product Principles

1. Show real work instead of describing it; every claim points at a project.
2. Give all three identities (IE, web dev, finance/data) equal footing.
3. A skimmer should understand who the owner is within seconds.
4. Honesty over polish: prototypes are labeled as MVPs or prototypes.
